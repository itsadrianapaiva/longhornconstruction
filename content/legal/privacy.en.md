## What we collect and why

- **Contact details** (name, email, phone, message) when you contact us.  
  Purpose: reply to your request, give quotes, and manage pre-contract steps.  
  Lawful basis: contract or pre-contract steps (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) and legitimate interests (art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Technical logs** (IP address, user agent, timestamps, basic error and access logs).  
  Purpose: security, fraud and abuse prevention, service reliability.  
  Lawful basis: legitimate interests (art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Cookies**  
  We use only strictly necessary cookies for core site functions. If we add analytics or marketing cookies later, we will request prior consent and provide controls.

## Retention

- Contact messages: up to **24 months** after the last interaction, unless needed longer for contracts, warranties or legal claims.
- Security logs: typically **6 months**, or longer if needed for incident investigation.
- Invoices and accounting records: kept for periods required by law.

## Processors and transfers

We use service providers for hosting, email and reliability that process data under our instructions. If data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability. When processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. You can lodge a complaint with the Portuguese DPA (**CNPD**).

## How to contact us

Email **info@longhorn.construction**. We aim to reply within one month under GDPR.
